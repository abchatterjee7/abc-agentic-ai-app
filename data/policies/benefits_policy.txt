Title: Employee Benefits Policy

Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered by group health insurance from their first day, with a sum insured of 500,000 per year. Coverage extends to the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. Parents can be added at the employee's own cost during the annual enrolment window.

Retirement and Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of basic salary to the provident fund, matching the employee's own contribution.

Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 30,000 for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Requests above this amount need director approval. Certifications are reimbursed after successful completion.

Wellness and Meals
A monthly wellness allowance of 2,000 can be claimed for gym memberships, fitness classes or mental-health apps. Free lunch is provided on office days. The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling — 6 sessions per year per employee.

Performance Bonus
Performance bonuses are paid annually in April based on company and individual performance ratings, with a target of 8% to 15% of annual base salary depending on grade.
