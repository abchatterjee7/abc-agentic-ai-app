Title: Leave Policy

Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; any balance above that expires on 31 March. Leave requests should be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager. Leave of more than 10 consecutive days needs 30 days' notice and department head approval.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

Maternity, Paternity and Adoption Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth. Adoption leave follows the same entitlement as maternity or paternity leave, depending on the primary caregiver.

Casual and Public Holidays
Employees get 6 days of casual leave per year for urgent personal matters, with same-day notice to the manager. The company observes 12 public holidays per year; the list is published each January.

Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted for study, family emergencies or personal reasons, subject to HR and department head approval.
