Title: Travel and Expense Policy

Submitting Expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense through the finance portal, with original receipts for every item above 500. Claims submitted after 60 days will be rejected. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

Travel
Flights must be booked at least 14 days ahead in economy class through the approved travel portal. Hotel stays are capped at 8,000 per night in metro cities and 5,000 per night elsewhere. Business-class travel needs VP approval and is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours.

Daily Allowance
While travelling for work, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 1,500 for domestic trips and 50 USD for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment must be pre-approved by the manager and must include the names of all attendees and the business purpose. The limit is 3,000 per person.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, minibar charges, upgrades not approved in advance and family members' travel costs are not reimbursable.
