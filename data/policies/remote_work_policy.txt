Title: Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Hybrid Schedule
Employees are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week. Teams choose their anchor days, typically Tuesday to Thursday. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head and HR.

Working From Another City or Country
Working remotely from a different city within the country is allowed for up to 20 days per year with manager approval. Working from another country is allowed for a maximum of 15 days per calendar year, requires approval from the manager and HR at least 30 days in advance, and is subject to tax, immigration and data-security checks. Employees must never work from another country without approval.

Core Hours and Availability
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees must be reachable during core hours and keep their calendar up to date.

Home Office Setup
A one-time home-office allowance of 15,000 is provided for equipment such as a chair, monitor or desk. Internet reimbursement of up to 1,000 per month is available for employees working remotely at least 2 days per week.

Security Requirements
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the corporate VPN. Public Wi-Fi must not be used for work without the VPN. Confidential documents must not be stored on personal devices.
